# improvement-loop.md

> How we improve over time without burning out on optimization.

---

## The Rule

**Suggest. Don't auto-apply.**

Every Sunday, after pulling weekly numbers, the agent may produce up to **3 improvement suggestions**, in this exact format:

```
=== הצעות לשיפור (לא הוטמעו) ===
1. [Suggestion]: [data point that supports it]
2. ...
3. ...
```

Dor reviews. If he says yes → the agent applies in the next post. If he ignores or says no → the suggestion is discarded.

---

## Categories of Suggestions

The agent looks at three things only:

### 1. How to grow views
Examples:
- "Pattern A WhatsApp messages had 1.4× clicks vs Pattern B in the last 4 weeks. Default to Pattern A."
- "Posts where Wave #1 was a story (not a thought) had 2× shares. Consider biasing toward stories."

### 2. How to grow median time on page
Examples:
- "Posts with embedded video had 38s longer median time. Always include Wave #5 video."
- "Posts longer than 1,200 words had lower completion. Cap at 1,000."

### 3. How to grow CTA click-through
Examples:
- "Trip CTA clicks dropped 60% in Pattern X — try a softer wording."
- "Newsletter CTA always under-performs WhatsApp CTA. Consider removing newsletter CTA from posts where audience is already in the WhatsApp group."

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- ❌ Long lists of "10 ways to improve" — keep it to 3
- ❌ Suggestions based on a single week of data — wait for 3+ weeks of pattern
- ❌ Suggestions that change voice (those are not "improvements" — they're rewrites)
- ❌ Suggestions that require new tools or paid software
- ❌ Suggesting *more* posting frequency — weekly is the cadence, locked

---

## What Dor Decides, Not the Agent

- Voice changes
- Adding new content sections (e.g., a "Wave #6")
- Removing constants (Hero, Intro, fixed CTAs)
- Changing publishing day (Friday is the rhythm)

The agent's loop is mechanical. Strategy stays with Dor.
